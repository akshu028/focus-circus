🎪 Focus Circus::

  |Turn your study sessions into a performance.
  
Focus Circus is a fun, circus-themed productivity and 
focus timer that turns studying into a series of exciting acts.
Instead of a boring Pomodoro timer, every study session becomes a performance where you choose your act, 
focus, take an intermission, and celebrate your achievements.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
✨ Features::

🎭 Choose Your Act – Select different focus durations for your study session.

⏱️ Focus Timer – Stay focused with a visually engaging countdown timer.

🎪 Custom Act Names – Name your study session or task.

🎉 Ovation Screen – Celebrate after completing a focus session.

🎊 Confetti Animation – Get rewarded for completing your act.

☕ Intermission Break – Take a 5-minute break between focus sessions.

📊 Statistics Dashboard – Track your productivity and focus history.

🔥 Focus Streaks – See how consistently you are studying.

📅 Monthly Calendar – View your focused study days.

🔊 Sound Control – Turn background sound on or off.

💾 Local Storage – Your session statistics are saved in your browser.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🎯 How It Works:

Enter the Focus Circus 🎪

Choose your focus duration.

Give your study session an Act Name.

Start the timer and focus.

Complete your act and receive an Ovation 🎉

Take an Intermission when needed.

Check your statistics and improve your consistency.

📊 Productivity Tracking:

Focus Circus tracks:

Total Showtime – Total minutes spent focusing.

Acts Done – Number of completed focus sessions.

Focus Streak – Consecutive days with focus activity.

Best Day – The day with the highest total focus time.

Monthly Calendar – Daily focus activity throughout the month.

  |☕ Intermission/break time is not included in productivity statistics.

Most productivity timers feel repetitive and boring.
